Training aid only. Verify every value against the THB, the POH and your aircraft's weight report before flight. One T/O data sheet per takeoff (its landing box is the emergency return to the same runway at T/O weight) and one for the landing at the destination. Your draft is saved in this browser; calculations are stored in your account. BEM and CG come from the fleet table by registration. Airport limits and notes: THB Appendix A (07 APR 2026); elevations: FAA. Written onto LAT form 1002; tables from THB SR20 Rev 06, linear interpolation (takeoff and landing distances use the next higher table weight).
